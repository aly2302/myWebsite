## Notes

- The playbook says never to take assets from another site or source without a license. The film
  clip predates this redesign and fits the incumbent world, but it's not licensed for a public
  site. Options: a self-made or AI-generated space loop, a CC0 stock clip (Pexels, Mixkit), or a
  code-built visual (the particle field already is one). Decision is the owner's.
- Motion level 2 doesn't require a hero asset. The particle canvas is the current hero visual and
  is code-built, so it has no license issue.
