## Product Principles

1. Get to "email him" fast: every section should leave a recruiter closer to writing.
2. Professional work first: the Closer role is the strongest proof and leads the story; projects and the publication show depth.
3. Show, don't list: describe what he built and delivered; skill lists are support, not the headline.
4. Frontend first, depth second: lead with frontend engineering, and use networking, infrastructure, security and AI as the differentiator, not as competing headlines.
5. Only real facts: no inflated titles, invented projects or numbers, and nothing confidential from client work.
