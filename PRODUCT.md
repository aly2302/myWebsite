## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers and tech leads deciding whether to interview Rodrigo Novelo for a junior or graduate role. They arrive from a CV, LinkedIn or an application, skim quickly, and need to judge in under a minute what he can do and whether to reach out.

## Product Purpose

The personal portfolio of Rodrigo Novelo, a Computer Engineering graduate (B.Sc., ISEC, 09/2021 to 10/2025) looking for his first full-time role. Success means a recruiter emails him at rodrigonovelo.pro@gmail.com. Downloading the CV, LinkedIn and GitHub are supporting actions.

## Positioning

He leads as a web developer. Behind that is something most junior web candidates can't truthfully claim: a networking and systems-administration degree, hands-on security tooling, and a peer-reviewed publication on personalised LLMs. He can build the front end and also understand the network, infrastructure and AI behind it.

## Operating Context

- Visitors usually come from a job application, CV link or LinkedIn profile, on desktop at work or on a phone between tasks.
- The site is a single static page; the CV PDF is the artifact that travels into hiring pipelines.
- Open to junior/graduate roles in Portugal, on-site or remote. Based in Redondo, Portugal.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Plain HTML, CSS and vanilla JS served with Vite; no frameworks (see CLAUDE.md).
- Sections today: home, technical skills, projects ("Systems"), timeline ("Log": education and work), blog placeholder, footer.
- The blog stays as a "coming soon" placeholder until posts exist. No invented posts.
- Web development is the lead field, but no standalone web projects exist yet beyond the email assistant's web dashboard and this site. Don't add projects until they're real. Open decision: how to make the web-dev lead credible with current evidence.

## Product Principles

1. Get to "email him" fast: every section should leave a recruiter closer to writing.
2. Show, don't list: the project, the publication and the site's own craft are the proof; skill lists are support.
3. Web first, depth second: lead with web development, and use networking, security and AI as the differentiator, not as competing headlines.
4. Only real facts: no inflated titles, invented projects or numbers.
